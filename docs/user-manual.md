# Alumnet User Manual

Welcome to the Alumnet platform. This guide will provide you with step-by-step instruction for navigating the web application, managing your profile and engaging with the alumni community.

---

## 1. Getting Started 

Follow these initial steps to activate your account and establish your presence within the network.

### 1.1 Registration 
* Navigate to the main portal.
* Enter your verified institutional email address to create your Alumnet credentials.
* Verify your email via confirmation link sent to the email inbox.

### 1.2 Profile Configuration
**Avatar:** Upload a professional headshot picture.
**Academic Details:** Input details such as your graduation year and major.
**Professional Details:** List your current profession, industry and company.  

### 1.3 Secure Authentication
* Log in using your registered email address and password. 
* Ensure your session is securely established before navigating away from the portal.

---

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard

The primary interface serves as your central hub for community updates, professional opportunities and network news. 

* **Home Feed:** View recent announcement, job postings and articles shared by fellow alumni and university administrators. 
* **Alumni Directory:** Utilizes the search interface to filter members by graduation year, major, geographic location or current industry.
* **Events Calender:** Access the integrated calendar to view upcoming webinars, regional meetups and networking events. Click on any event to RSVP directly.

---

## 3. Connecting with Alumni 

Leverage the platform's communication tools to foster professional relationships, collaborate on projects and seek mentorship.

## 3.1 Direct Messaging 
Use the built-in communication system to send secure, private messages to individual alumni or established connections.

## 3.2 Connection Requests
Send digital networking requests to members you wish to add to your personal professional circle. Once accepted, you will gain access to their expanded profile details.

# 3.3 Interest Groups
Join specialized discussion forums based on specific career fields, share collegiate interests or regional alumni chapters to participate in targeted conversations. 

--- 

## 4. Account & Settings Management 

Maintain control over your data visibility, communication preferences and application security. 

## 4.1 Privacy Controls 
Adjust your visibility settings in the **settings** menu to determine which profile elements are public, restricted to connections or completely hidden from directory. 

## 4.2 Notification Preferences 
Toggle email and in-app alerts for: 
* New direct messages
* Accepted connection requests 
* Upcoming registered events 
* Group announcements

## 4.3 Technical Support
If you experience unexpected behaviour within the client interface, navigate to the **Help Center** tab to consult the FAQs or submit ticket to the administrative team.
